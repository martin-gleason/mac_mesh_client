### 1 · The fake's only clock is the sample count — `major`

`src/mesh_client/radio.py:213-233`, `tests/test_radio.py:29`.

`recover_after=n` means *n further calls to `snapshot()`*, whatever the caller's poll
schedule. `FR6` requires capped backoff, which by definition makes that schedule
non-uniform. Ran:

```
r = FakeRadio(); r.disconnect(recover_after=3)
polls at [30,30,30,30]  -> link back after 120s of wall time
polls at [1,2,4,8]      -> link back after  15s of wall time
```

At `recover_after=10` — the value `tests/test_radio.py` annotates as *"`D3` five minutes,
the number pass criterion 2 is evaluated against"* — a caller backing off `1,2,4,…,1024`
sees the link return after **2047 s = 34 minutes**, scored as recovered.

**Why it is major rather than cosmetic.** The plan's own `F1-T4` checkpoint is *"a fake
that drops at a known time and returns 90 seconds later."* There is no way to say that to
this fake. And an `F1-T4` implementation that evaluates *"restored within five minutes"* by
counting samples rather than consulting the injected clock **agrees with this fake
exactly**, because the fake's only clock is the sample count. That is a fixture satisfying
both the right and the wrong implementation — the tautology `conventions.md` names by that
name. It would be handed to the owner as the contract for a `tests only` task.

Same root cause: the radio's clock runs at an arbitrary rate against wall time. Under the
backoff above, uptime advanced **120 s while 15 s elapsed**, so the module's own
*"uptime jumped by the elapsed time"* discriminator is not available against this fake.

### 3 · A reboot that keeps its socket — `minor`

`src/mesh_client/radio.py:180-188`. `r.reboot()` steps uptime back and leaves
`connected=True`. A radio cannot reboot without dropping its link. `FR6` requires every
disconnect *and* reconnect to be its own event, so this fake can stage a reboot producing
**zero** connection events — and an `F1-T4` implementation that never pairs the two passes
against it.

### 7 · The seam has no reconnect affordance — `minor` · **owner**

`src/mesh_client/radio.py:235-238`. `reconnect()` is dead — replacing its body with
`raise NotImplementedError` leaves the suite green. And `Radio.__protocol_attrs__ ==
['snapshot']`, so `reconnect()` is **off-protocol**: a real `TCPInterface` adapter is not
obliged to provide one.

`F1-T4` is *"reconnect, backoff, and the event log"*, and the only reconnect strategy this
seam permits is *keep calling `snapshot()` until `connected` flips*. **That is `F1-T4`'s
central arrangement, decided in `F1-T1`, for a task the ladder puts at `tests only`.** The
plan accepts that cost explicitly for `F1-T2` and says `F1-T3` onward drop the scaffold
precisely to avoid it. This is the dial cost, and it landed one task further on than the
plan authorised.

### 8 · Sampler state in the seam — `minor`

`src/mesh_client/radio.py:78`. `position_packets` is the only field never `None`, is
cumulative-for-the-run rather than an observation, and survives disconnects. A connected
radio with no traffic gives `[0,0,0]`; a disconnected radio that had seen four packets
gives `[4,4,4]` — **identical delta sequences**, separated only by `connected`. Recoverable,
but every consumer must diff *and* consult a second field, and `F1-T2`'s record inherits
the asymmetry.
